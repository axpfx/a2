01 — The Main Objective

To architect a cohesive and authoritative brand identity that builds absolute trust with restaurant owners and procurement specialists.

Strategic Implementation

Our agency employs a methodical process to extract the core essence of your operation. We focus on refining the communication framework, ensuring that every visual touchpoint—from digital presence to physical distribution—speaks the language of quality and reliability.

Market Positioning

By maintaining rigorous visual consistency across distribution trucks, packaging, and digital channels, we reinforce the brand's promise of professionalism. This builds a psychological bridge of trust between you and the elite restaurateurs of Iraq.

02 — Work Methodology

The Agency Process.

01

Discovery Session

An initial deep-dive meeting to go through a strategic questionnaire. We aim to gain information on the essence, vision, and core values of Tariq Al-Afia to ensure the brand reflects its true growth trajectory.

02

Visual Identity Creation

Armed with market research, we develop two initial logo concepts and a visual style foundation. One concept is refined based on feedback to create core elements like colors, typography, and iconography.

03

Brand Guidelines

All developed material is consolidated into a comprehensive master document. This includes directions and guides on how to apply the branding consistently across all future distribution channels.
